ABOUT REPAIRLENS AI

Understand the problem. Decide with clarity.

RepairLens AI is an AI-powered repair decision assistant designed to help users understand device problems before deciding what to do next.

◈

WHAT YOU CAN SHARE

Give your device a little context.

Users can provide device details, describe the problem, and upload an image of the damaged device or area.

✦

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Clearer options before the next step.

RepairLens AI provides possible problem identification, possible causes, image evidence, confidence, estimated cost, repair-or-replacement guidance, safe troubleshooting, and professional repair guidance.

Built for better repair decisionsUnderstand a problem before approaching a repair professional→

The goal of RepairLens AI is to make repair decisions easier, clearer, and more informed. It can also help users compare repair options and prepare better questions for a qualified technician.

⚠️
Important limitation

AI results are estimates and possible causes, not guaranteed technical diagnoses. Some problems cannot be identified from an image and may require professional inspection.
